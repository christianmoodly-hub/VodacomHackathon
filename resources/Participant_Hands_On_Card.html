Step 2 — Create your Amazon Quick account
4
Search for Amazon Quick in the AWS Console
Type Quick in the search bar at the top of the AWS Console, or find it under Analytics. Click the result to open the Amazon Quick sign-up page.
5
Enter an account name
Choose any account name you like — this is just a label for your Quick workspace.
✓ Can be anything — e.g. your team name
6
Enter a notification email address
Enter any email address — this does not have to be your university or personal address.
✓ Any email address works
7
Click "Create account"
Leave all other settings as they are. Review your choices and click Create account.
8
Click "Go to Amazon Quick"
Your Quick account is ready. Click Go to Amazon Quick to open it.
✓ You're in — Quick Web is open
